# Clinical research terminology

Use the official terms of the output language. Do not use outdated terms even if the user does; you may acknowledge the user's word once ("研究中心, i.e. 试验现场"). This applies everywhere, **including when restating the user's timeline and inside tables** (the user's "研究医生" becomes 助理研究者, or 主要研究者 if that is who it was).

**Order of authority**

- **Chinese**: (1) the text of China's GCP, 2026 revision (NMPA, effective 2026-09-01); (2) the CDE Chinese translation of the ICH E6(R3) glossary, for terms the GCP text does not define (GCP 2026 Article 54 points to it). Where they differ, the GCP text prevails. (3) Added 2026-11-09: for terms that (1) and (2) do not cover, the Chinese text of the document concerned in [regulations/](regulations/README.md) (CDE Chinese versions of E2A, E8(R1), E9, E9(R1); Chinese laws and rules), cited by file and section. Where such a text uses a different word for a concept that (1) or (2) covers, use the (1)/(2) term in your own Chinese and quote the other text in its own words (see §6).
- **English**: the ICH E6(R3) final glossary.
- **Other languages**: the official translation used by that country's regulator if you know it; otherwise the English E6(R3) term with a short explanation in the user's language.

Known Chinese differences: Investigator Site = **试验现场** (GCP 2026 text), not "研究者现场" (CDE glossary). SUSAR = **可疑且非预期严重不良反应** (with "且"). Also (checked 2026-11-09): investigational product = **临床试验用药品** (GCP 2026 text), not "试验用药品" (CDE glossary); IRB/IEC = **伦理审查委员会** (GCP 2026 text), while the CDE glossary heading is "机构审查委员会（IRB）/独立伦理委员会（IEC）". Chinese laws and the other CDE ICH translations use further variants (受试者, 监护人, 伦理委员会, 研究药物): see §6.

## 4. Formats

- Keep study numbers, version numbers, and document codes exactly as given.
- Dates: Chinese 2026年9月1日 (no leading zeros, as in the GCP text); English DD MMM YYYY (for example 28 May 2026).
- Spell out an abbreviation the first time it appears in a report.
